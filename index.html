Property Overview

Mediterranean
Masterpiece

Set behind private gates on one of Miami's most coveted streets, 5100 SW 86th Street is a Mediterranean estate unlike anything on the market. This ivy-draped home sits on nearly 30,000 square feet of lush tropical grounds in the heart of Ponce Davis — offering six bedrooms, five baths, and over 7,250 square feet of total living space designed for those who demand both grandeur and privacy.

From the soaring marble foyer to the twin 10-foot deep heated pools framed by towering palms, every detail of this home was built to impress.

Location

Ponce Davis —
Miami's Most Prestigious Address

Ponce Davis sits quietly between Coconut Grove and Coral Gables — two of Miami's most beloved neighborhoods — offering the rare combination of true residential tranquility and city-close convenience. Wide streets, a canopy of mature trees, and neighbors who value privacy make this one of the few places in South Florida that genuinely feels like an escape.
